# Content Audit

Before sentence-level polishing, verify:

- every prompt component is answered;
- the thesis is clear and supported;
- evidence is specific and relevant;
- chronology and causality are accurate;
- ownership verbs match the applicant's role;
- technical claims and results are not overstated;
- program-fit details are correct and connected to a real need;
- career goals follow from the evidence;
- repeated material performs distinct work;
- the document fits the required length.

Resolve content failures before humanization. Sentence polish cannot compensate for a missing answer or unsupported claim.
