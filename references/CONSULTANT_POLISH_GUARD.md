# Consultant Polish Guard

Check whether the draft sounds more strategically mature, rhetorically controlled, or self-interpreting than the applicant's genuine samples support.

Watch for perfect causal arcs, flawless transitions, unusually elevated abstractions, compressed insights the applicant has not expressed elsewhere, professional branding language, and every experience resolving into the program's ideal candidate profile.

Apply an upgrade budget: grammar and clarity may improve substantially; rhetorical maturity, vocabulary, and conceptual synthesis should remain within a credible extension of the applicant's voice. Reduce over-polish through direct syntax, concrete observations, honest limits, and fewer explanatory conclusions. Never simulate authenticity with mistakes.
