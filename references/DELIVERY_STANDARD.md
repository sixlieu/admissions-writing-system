# Delivery Standard

A finished draft is truthful, prompt-responsive, logically coherent, evidence-based, program-specific where required, within the word limit, and believable in the applicant's voice. It has no material ownership inflation, fabricated fit, obvious AI architecture, or consultant over-polish.

Reopen a delivered draft for a factual error, logical problem, prompt mismatch, program-fit error, or important new material. Treat detector-score fluctuations and requests for vague sophistication as diagnostic prompts, not automatic reasons for a full rewrite.

Document unresolved verification needs. Do not represent an unchecked draft as ready to submit.
