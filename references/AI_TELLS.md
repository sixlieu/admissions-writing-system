# AI and Admissions Tells

Review patterns in context rather than banning individual words.

Common signals include generic transformation claims; abstract openings detached from evidence; perfectly symmetrical paragraph structures; repeated three-part lists; uniform sentence length; forced uplift at every paragraph ending; excessive transition phrases; and stock language such as pivotal, transformative, deepened my understanding, at the intersection of, bridge theory and practice, or not only...but also.

Repair the underlying problem. Replace abstraction with supported detail, vary structure according to meaning, remove redundant summaries, and state conclusions at the level the evidence earns. Do not substitute a new set of fashionable phrases.
