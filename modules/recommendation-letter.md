# Recommendation Letter

Write from the recommender's credible vantage point. Establish relationship, duration, setting, and comparison group before making strong evaluations.

Support praise with observed behavior and consequences. A recommender may infer qualities from what they witnessed, but should not narrate the student's private inner journey like an SOP.

Match the recommender's likely tone and expertise. Avoid identical voice across letters, exhaustive resumes, and claims the recommender could not know.

Keep comparison statements precise. Do not invent rankings, percentiles, institutional authority, or personal anecdotes.
