# Essay Architecture

State the essay's core thesis in one sentence. It should explain what the evidence means, not merely name an interest.

Design paragraphs by function: establish the question, supply evidence, show reasoning or change, connect preparation to the next step, and answer fit or goals when required. Each paragraph should advance the same argument while doing distinct work.

Check causality. Do not convert chronology into a false progression or claim that one event transformed the applicant without evidence. Let turning points emerge from decisions, consequences, or revised assumptions.

Avoid a mechanical sequence in which every paragraph repeats claim, anecdote, lesson, and uplift. Vary paragraph shape according to what the evidence needs.
