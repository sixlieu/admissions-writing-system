# Research Writing

Describe the research question, why it mattered, the applicant's actual role, methods used, important decisions, evidence or results, limitations, and the next question.

Preserve technical precision while explaining significance to the likely reader. Do not turn team output into individual ownership. Use verbs that match the contribution exactly.

Include failed approaches, debugging, boundary conditions, or changes in interpretation when they reveal research judgment. Do not manufacture a clean success arc.

Separate completed findings from hypotheses and future interests. Never imply causality, generalizability, or statistical strength beyond the evidence.
