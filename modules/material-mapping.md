# Material Mapping

Inventory only user-supplied or verified material. For each item record: event, applicant action, obstacle or decision, result, reflection, relevance to the prompt, and confidence in the facts.

Prefer material that shows a choice, tradeoff, change in method, or earned insight. Specific ordinary evidence is stronger than grand unsupported claims.

Distinguish:

- firsthand action from observation;
- individual work from team work;
- measured results from impressions;
- contemporary motivation from a retrospective narrative;
- confirmed facts from details that need verification.

Do not force every experience into the essay. Select the smallest set that can support the central claim without repetition.
