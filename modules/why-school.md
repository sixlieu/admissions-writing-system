# Why School

Use program details only when they change what the applicant can learn, investigate, build, or contribute. Explain the link between a verified resource and a specific preparation gap or next question.

Avoid lists of courses, faculty, centers, rankings, location advantages, and website language. One well-explained fit link is worth more than several namedrops.

Verify current faculty affiliation, course availability, lab or center names, degree structure, and special tracks when possible. If verification is unavailable, flag the claim rather than guessing.

Show mutual fit with restraint. Contribution claims need evidence from the applicant's prior work or perspective.
