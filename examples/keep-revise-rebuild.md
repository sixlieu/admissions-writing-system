# KEEP / REVISE / REBUILD Example

**KEEP**

`I became interested in why eligible families still failed to receive benefits.`

It is clear, natural, and specific enough for its function.

**REVISE**

Revise a sentence when its evidence is sound but the wording is generic, repetitive, or out of voice. State what changed and preserve every lock.

**REBUILD**

Rebuild a paragraph that claims the applicant led a study when the source says they assisted with data cleaning. Restore the true role first, then redesign the paragraph around the judgment that role actually demonstrates.
