# Delivery Tests

Confirm the final version answers the exact prompt, meets the word or character limit, contains no unresolved silent guesses, and preserves the required document type and format.

Read once as an admissions reviewer: identify the central claim, evidence, preparation, next step, and fit. Read once as the applicant: flag any sentence they would not understand, endorse, or plausibly say.

Delivery fails if either reading reveals a material mismatch.
