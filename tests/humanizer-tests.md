# Humanizer Tests

Test the humanized version against the pre-edit draft and genuine writing samples.

- The thesis and all locks are unchanged.
- Strong natural sentences were allowed to remain.
- Revisions reduce generic structure or phrasing for a clear reason.
- Voice remains a credible extension of the applicant.
- No deliberate errors, random synonym swaps, or fake anecdotes appear.
- The result reads coherently without optimizing for a detector score.

If a revision is harder to defend than the original, restore the original.
